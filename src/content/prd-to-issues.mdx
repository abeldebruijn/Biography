The `prd-to-issues` skill turns a product requirements document into concrete work items. Its focus is on splitting a broad plan into smaller slices that can be owned independently and delivered incrementally.

In practice, use it once a PRD is stable and you want to move from planning into execution. It is useful for transforming product language into issue-sized engineering tasks without losing the structure of the original feature.

## Install
```
npx skills add mattpocock/skills@prd-to-issues
```
